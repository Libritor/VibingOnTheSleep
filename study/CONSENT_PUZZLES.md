# Information sheet: puzzles and quiet sounds during sleep

MannLab Canada and the University of Toronto, Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering.
Investigator: Alexander Vicol. Supervisor: Prof. Steve Mann.
Version 0.1, 28 October 2026. Not yet reviewed by a Research Ethics Board. Until an REB approval
number is on file, only the investigators may take part, as a self-experiment.

## What the study asks

Whether a puzzle you could not solve in the evening is more likely to be solved the next morning
if its sound is played quietly while you are in deep sleep. This repeats the design of Sanders,
Osburn, Paller and Beeman (Psychological Science, 2019), who found 31.7% of cued puzzles solved
the next morning against 20.5% of uncued ones, with engineering puzzles in place of theirs.

## What happens in one night

1. **Evening, at the computer, up to about 30 minutes.** You try about 20 puzzles (arithmetic,
   resistor networks, anagrams), up to 90 seconds each. Each puzzle has its own short sound,
   which plays while the puzzle is on screen.
2. **Bedtime.** You put on a Muse headband. A quiet noise plays, getting louder, and you press a
   key as soon as you hear it; this sets how loud the night's sounds are.
3. **Night.** The computer watches your brain activity. When it sees deep (slow-wave) sleep, it
   plays the sounds of half of the puzzles you did not solve, very quietly, every 10 to 14 seconds.
   If it sees signs of you waking or moving, it stops at once and turns the volume down. The
   other half are never played. You are not told which half is which until the morning.
4. **Morning, at the computer, up to about 20 minutes.** You try the unsolved puzzles again,
   without sounds. You are asked whether you heard anything in the night and how you slept.
   Then you are shown which puzzles were played.

## Risks

The sounds are set close to your hearing threshold, so most people do not wake or remember them.
They can still wake you or make your sleep lighter. If they do, say so in the morning and the
volume will be lowered, or stop the study. The headband is a consumer product with dry
electrodes and no electrical stimulation. Do not take part if you have a diagnosed sleep disorder,
epilepsy or a history of seizures, are taking sedating medication, or are pregnant.

## Your data

- Everything is stored under a pseudonym (for example P-0003) on the investigator's computer.
- Recorded: the headband's EEG channels and accelerometer all night, the computer's sleep
  estimates, the time and loudness of every sound played, your typed puzzle answers and their
  timing, and your morning answers about the night.
- No audio of you is recorded. Nothing goes to any website, cloud service or blockchain.
- Nothing is published that could identify you.

## Withdrawing

You can stop any night by taking the headband off, and leave the study at any time without giving
a reason. You can ask for all of your data to be deleted.

## This is not medical care

The rig is a research instrument, not a medical device, and it does not diagnose or treat anything.
